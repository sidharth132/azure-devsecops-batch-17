## 📌 1. Overview: Agile in DevOps Teams

Agile is an iterative approach to project management and software development that helps teams deliver value faster and with greater predictability:

```
 Product Backlog  --->  Sprint Planning  --->  Sprint Backlog  --->  Sprint Execution (2 Weeks)  --->  Sprint Review & Retrospective
 (All Epics/Stories)     (Select deliverables)  (Committed items)    (Daily Standup Meetings)         (Demo & Continuous Improvement)
```

---

## 👥 2. Core Scrum Roles & Artifacts

* **Product Owner:** Defines product vision, prioritizes the Product Backlog, and represents business stakeholders.
* **Scrum Master:** Facilitates ceremonies, removes impediments, and ensures Agile practices are followed.
* **Development / DevOps Team:** Cross-functional engineers responsible for executing stories and delivering working increments.
* **Ceremonies:**
  * **Sprint Planning:** Commit to stories for the upcoming 2-week cycle.
  * **Daily Standup (15 mins):** Three questions: *What did you do yesterday? What will you do today? Are there any blockers?*
  * **Sprint Review / Demo:** Showcase completed deliverables to stakeholders.
  * **Sprint Retrospective:** Discuss process improvements (*What went well? What needs improvement?*).

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways
1. In DevOps interviews, articulate your day-to-day work within the Agile Scrum framework using Azure Boards or Jira.
2. Highlight how infrastructure tasks are tracked as User Stories, Spikes, and Bug Fixes with clear acceptance criteria.
